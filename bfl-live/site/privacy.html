This notice explains what information Built For Life UK ("we", "Jory") collects when you use this website, the "Future You" questionnaire, checkout and the client portal, why we use it, and your rights under UK data protection law (UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

Who we are: Built For Life UK, [REGISTERED BUSINESS NAME AND ADDRESS]. Contact: hello@builtforlife.uk. We are the data controller.

What we collect

  • Contact details: your name, email and mobile number.
  • Your answers: age, sex, height, weight, target weight, training experience, where you train, diet, sleep, goal, and wedding date (Bridal Bootcamp).
  • Photos: the photo you add to the questionnaire, check-in photos, and photos you send in chat.
  • Coaching records: workouts and the sets you log, food, water and habit logs, weekly check-ins (weight, energy, sleep, notes), session bookings, and messages, photos and videos you send your coach.
  • Health questionnaire: your answers to our pre-exercise health questions, any details you add, your emergency contact, and your signed agreement.
  • Community: if you take part, your first name and initial on challenge leaderboards, and anything you post on the members' wall. You can hide yourself at any time in your portal.
  • Payments: which plan you bought, the amount and date. Card details are handled by Stripe and never reach us.
  • Login and the app: your email and a one-time code. We set one essential cookie to keep you logged in, and the app keeps a copy of your plan on your phone so it works offline (removed when you log out). If you turn on notifications, we store a notification address for your device. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Weight, body photos and similar details can be health data, which the law treats as special category data. We only use it with your explicit consent, which you give in the questionnaire, and you can withdraw it at any time.

The AI preview

If you add a photo, it's sent to Google's Gemini image service to create a realistic preview of you at your target. Only your photo and a fixed instruction made from your numbers (current weight, target and plan length) are sent, never your name or contact details. The preview is an illustration of a goal, not a promise of results. If you'd rather not use AI, skip the photo: you'll see a simple preview instead.

Who we share it with

We never sell your data. We use these providers to run the service, under contracts that protect your data:

  • Cloudflare: website hosting, database and photo storage.
  • Stripe: card payments.
  • Google (Gemini): creating the AI preview from your photo.
  • Resend: sending login codes and emails.
  • Open Food Facts: when you search for a food or scan a barcode, only the search words or barcode number are sent (never who you are).
  • Google, Apple and Mozilla push services: deliver notifications to your device. They see only the encrypted notification.
  • WhatsApp (Meta): only if you choose to message Jory there.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, they use legal safeguards approved for UK data, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

How long we keep it

  • Questionnaire answers and photos from people who don't join: up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • Client coaching records and photos: for your programme, then up to 12 months after it ends, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Payment and invoice records: 6 years, as required for tax.
  • Health questionnaires: for your programme and up to 12 months after, so your coach has the history if you return.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or get a copy to take elsewhere. You can withdraw consent at any time. Email hello@builtforlife.uk and we'll reply within one month. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Keeping it safe

Your data is stored on secure servers. Photos can only be viewed by you and your coach when logged in. Logins use one-time codes sent to your email, so there's no password to steal.
